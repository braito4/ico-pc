## New

- On a wide screen, the grainy veil over the picture during Ico's vision now covers the whole picture, out to the edges.
- Photo mode has one more film effect, Deja vu: the grainy veil and the flickering film scratches of Ico's vision, over any place you take a picture of. Your saved pictures include it.
- The menus' lettering has a soft, grainy dark halo round the letters, like the game's own menu lettering, instead of a blocky one.
- With Resolution above 1x, the game's glow, blur, shadow and fog effects look soft instead of showing a fine grid. At 1x they look as before.
- When the graphics card stops responding, the log now lists what the game was drawing at that moment, and the message suggests setting an overclocked card back to its stock clocks.

## Fixed

- When the graphics card stops responding, the message saying so now stays until you close it, instead of the game closing it after a minute as if the game had stopped responding.
