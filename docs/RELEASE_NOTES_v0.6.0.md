## New

- The touch controls on phones and tablets are laid out like a PlayStation controller and use the game's own button pictures. The buttons have the same real size on every screen, never cover each other, and keep clear of a foldable's fold.
- Options > Gameplay > East Reflector door fix (off by default): Ico can lead Yorda through the doorway at the top of the ladder instead of being sent back out.
- F12 now saves a zip file with everything a bug report needs, ready to attach.
- The renderer was reworked so that wide screens and every resolution are drawn natively, with the picture looking as the original did out of the box.
- A new Anti-aliasing setting (supersampling or MSAA) under Options > Display. The picture rows (Full-height picture, Texture filtering and Brightness) moved to Options > Effects.

## Fixed

- On the Save screen, the mouse can now pick an empty slot to save in, as the gamepad can.
- At wide aspect ratios the sea no longer loses a patch at the edge of the picture.
- The waterfall no longer shows a seam or lighter sides beside the picture.
- The opening forest no longer shows a dark shape in front of the camera, at any aspect ratio.
- The main menu's shading and the start-up signs now cover the whole wide picture.
- The picture was brighter than the PS2: the fog, the glow and every blended surface now match the original, pixel for pixel.
- The picture sat half a pixel low.
- The start-up sign no longer stutters.
- Supersampling no longer multiplies a picture that is already larger than the screen.
- The achievements pop-up switch is at the top of its list.
