## New

- Options > Extras > Add to Steam (Windows and Linux): adds the game to your Steam library with one choice.
- A portable download for Windows and Linux keeps your saves and settings in a folder beside the game, and the normal downloads come with a make-portable script that does the same.
- Options > Gameplay > Natural chains (off by default): the two chains that come down for Ico, at the Stone Pillar and by the water wheel, keep their links the right size the whole way down.

## Fixed

- The main gate's glow no longer disappears with anti-aliasing on, and the Anti-aliasing row shows the setting in use.
- The Film effect now covers the whole picture on wide screens, and keeps moving in photo mode.
- The picture at 16:9 is sharp again.
- Walls, shadows and effects no longer vanish at the edges of wide screens or while the camera moves, such as the water-tower lift wall, the courtyard wall by the sunbeams and the walls at 48:9.
- Motion is smooth with a frame-rate limit equal to your screen's refresh rate, and the 144 and 240 limits now reach their rate.
- Photo mode's right stick no longer rolls the camera after a steep shot.
- The boat cutscene keeps its sounds after the opening movie was skipped early.
- The touch controls show during movies on Android, and Start skips a movie on every input.
